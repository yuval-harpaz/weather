GWh (gigawatt-hours) is an amount of energy. Average GW is that energy spread evenly over the hours in the bar — the steady power it would take to produce it, comparable with the peak-demand figures on the reserve page. Solar includes Noga's estimate of rooftop and other distribution-connected panels, plus two solar-thermal plants. Liquid fuel is diesel and heavy fuel oil, burned mostly when gas supply is cut. Pumped storage counts only what it generates, not the power it uses to pump; batteries are not in this feed. The steam stages of combined-cycle plants carry no fuel label in the source and are counted with the fuel their unit is registered for.

Source: Half-hourly generation per unit published by Noga under its reporting obligation, one file per month, summed per day by code/grid_mix_update.py. Download the daily table · All charts
